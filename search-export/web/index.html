      A fieldset is a named list of columns that saved views use. Read live from your tenant. To back them up or copy them to another tenant, use  in Saved views.

        Connect to your tenant
        
    
        The key is sent once to the app running on this computer and kept in its memory for this session only. It is never saved to disk, in the browser, or shown again.
        
    
        To keep the key out of the browser entirely, start the app with the key already in its environment: ABSTRACT_API_KEY, or ABSTRACT_API_KEY_COMMAND to read it from your secret manager or keychain. It then connects on its own.
